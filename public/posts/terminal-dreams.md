I write best when the cursor blinks like a heartbeat.

Logs scroll, builds pass, tests fail—then pass again. The rhythm is familiar, almost comforting.

Somewhere between a warning and a green checkmark, the story of the project becomes the story of the person.

Tonight's goal: one more refactor, one less dependency, and a cleaner path forward.
